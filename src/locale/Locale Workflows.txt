Locales Workflows:

WebUI Locales:

After adding strings to HTML/JS files (use `data-i18n="btn_load_index"` inside tags):

  py www_tool.py * --translate --fast --clean --sort --newkeys

www_newkeys.json will be created showing the English of all strings added to the .json files.

This will pull out a specific translation for proofreading to the console or a file:

  py www_tool.py ru_RU --extract
  py www_tool.py ru_RU --extract checknewkeys.txt

Post both files for translators.

Put a translator's work back into changes.json and this will merge their changes:

  py www_tool.py ru_RU --merge changes.json --clean --sort

Don't forget to rebuild the wwwlocale.h file with:

  py make_wwwlocale.py


Display Locale Workflows are pretty similar:

  py display_tool.py * --translate --fast --clean --sort --newkeys

  py display_tool.py ru_RU --extract
  py display_tool.py ru_RU --extract checknewkeys.txt

  py display_tool.py ru_RU --merge changes.json --clean --sort

  py make_dsplocale.py
